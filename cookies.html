Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

binder.red does not use cookies. That's why you didn't see a cookie banner.

What this means

  • No cookies — neither technical nor analytics nor advertising cookies are set by this website.
  • No local storage — the website and the interactive demo don't save anything in your browser's storage. Whatever you do in the demo is forgotten when you leave the page.
  • No trackers — no analytics, advertising pixels, social-media plugins or session recording.
  • No third-party resources — fonts and icons are served from binder.red, so loading a page does not contact Google Fonts or other third-party services.

Because nothing is stored on or read from your device beyond what is strictly needed to deliver the page you requested, no consent is required under Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code and the Garante's cookie guidelines of 10 June 2021.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to external sources (for example the research cited on the home page) or, when we send one, to a scheduling service for booking a call. Those websites set their own cookies under their own policies once you visit them.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies or analytics, we will update this page and the Privacy Policy first, and ask for your consent before setting anything that requires it.
