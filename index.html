The problem

Fundraising still runs on cold email.

The information that decides whether a meeting is worth having — ticket, stage, sector, geography, timing — is the last thing either side finds out.

Founders

Hundreds of emails to find the few who write your cheque.

Lists give you names, not intent. You learn an investor doesn't do your stage after the third follow-up.

Investors

An inbox of decks that sit outside the thesis.

Sourcing means filtering noise. The companies that do fit arrive late, through whoever happened to forward them.

Both sides

Days of back-and-forth for a 20-minute call.

Even when interest is mutual, the scheduling, the intro and the agenda are all done by hand.

How it works

One app, two sides of the table.

  1. 01

    Say what you're raising.Set your ticket and thesis.

    Stage, amount, sector and traction on one card. Investors see exactly this — specific beats impressive, and it drives every match.

    Ticket range, lead or follow, stages and sectors. Only founders inside these lines ever reach your deck, and you can move them any time.

    • Explicit intent: what you want, what you offer, when you're free
    • Verified profiles on both sides
  2. 02

    Swipe on fit, not on hype.

    Every card leads with why you match — sector, stage, ticket, geography — before a word of pitch. Right is interested, left is pass.

    • A fit score you can read, not a black box
    • See reply time, founder rating and next free slot up front
    • ARR, growth and team on the card, deck after the match
  3. 03

    Double opt‑in. Nobody gets cold‑pitched.

    A match only exists when both sides said yes. There are no cold DMs and no ignored intros — and nobody is ever told they were passed on.

    • Intros unlock only on mutual interest
    • Binder already knows the slots you share
  4. 04

    The call books itself.

    Binder overlays both calendars and shows only the times you're both free. Pick one and it lands on both calendars, with an agenda drawn from both profiles.

    • 15, 20 or 30 minutes — a coffee chat, not a pitch marathon
    • Google Meet link and agenda created for you
  5. 05

    Follow up where the deal moves.

    Chat opens after the match, pinned to the booked call. Share the deck or the data room, bring in a co-investor, or reschedule — in one thread.

    • Deck shared only after both sides opted in
    • Quick replies move the deal forward

Why Binder

Fewer conversations. Better ones.

Binder is built on three rules. Each one removes a reason a fundraising conversation goes nowhere.

Intent, out loud.

Everyone states what they raise or write, at what stage, in which sectors and places — and when they're free. No guessing from a LinkedIn bio.

Fit before pitch.

Every card shows why you match before it shows anything else. You decide on the criteria that would have killed the deal anyway.

Yes means a meeting.

Two yeses unlock the intro and put a call in both calendars. The goal is a meeting actually held, not another message in an inbox.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Binder live?

We're running a pilot with a founding cohort of founders and investors. The pilot follows the same flow you see above — stated intent, fit, double opt-in, a booked call — with our team reviewing every match. Book a demo to see where the product is today.

Who can see my profile?

Only people on the other side whose criteria you fit. A founder appears only to investors whose ticket, stage and thesis include them; an investor appears only to founders they could actually fund. Your deck is shared only after both of you opt in.

Does anyone find out if I pass?

No. A pass is private. The other side only ever hears about a mutual yes.

How is this different from an investor database?

A database gives you names and leaves the outreach to you. Binder only surfaces people who fit, only connects you when both sides say yes, and turns that yes straight into a meeting on both calendars.

Which founders and investors is the pilot for?

Seed-stage startups that are raising now, and business angels and micro VCs who are actively writing cheques. We keep the first cohort in one coherent segment so every match has a real chance of being relevant.

How are profiles verified?

During the pilot, our team reviews every founder and investor before their profile goes live. Investors confirm the ticket they write and that they're deploying now; founders confirm what they're raising.
