Why Binder

Fewer conversations. Better ones.

Binder is built on three rules. Each one removes a reason a fundraising conversation goes nowhere.

Intent, out loud.

Everyone states what they raise or write, at what stage, in which sectors and markets — and when they're free. No guessing from a bio.

Fit before pitch.

Every card leads with why you match. You decide on the criteria that would have ended the conversation anyway.

Yes means a meeting.

Two yeses unlock the connection and put a call on both calendars. The goal is a meeting actually held, not another message.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Binder?

Binder is a founder–investor matchmaking app. Founders state what they are raising; investors state their ticket, stage, sectors and geography. Each side only sees the other where those overlap, a connection needs both sides to say yes, and Binder then books a 20-minute coffee chat into both calendars.

Is Binder live?

We're running a pilot with a founding cohort of founders and investors. The pilot follows the same flow you see above — stated intent, fit, mutual interest, a booked call — with our team reviewing every match. Book a demo to see where the product is today.

Can I try it?

Yes — the phones on this page run the actual prototype. Swipe, connect, book a slot, edit a profile. Nothing you do there is sent anywhere. Open it full screen; it works best on a phone.

Who can see my profile?

Only people on the other side whose criteria you fit, and you choose the visibility: relevant matches, connections only, or paused. Founders appear only to investors whose ticket, stage and thesis include them.

Does anyone find out if I pass?

No. A pass is private. The other side only ever hears about a mutual yes.

How is this different from an investor database?

A database gives you names and leaves the outreach to you. Binder only surfaces people who fit, only connects you when both sides say yes, and turns that yes straight into a meeting on both calendars.

Which founders and investors is the pilot for?

Seed-stage startups that are raising now, and business angels and micro VCs who are actively writing cheques. We keep the first cohort in one coherent segment so every match has a real chance of being relevant.

How are profiles verified?

During the pilot, our team reviews every founder and investor before their profile goes live. Investors confirm the ticket they write and that they're deploying now; founders confirm what they're raising.
