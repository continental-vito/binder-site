3. The interactive demo

The phones on this website run a prototype of the Binder app. Everything you type or tap in it stays in your browser and disappears when you leave the page; nothing is sent to us. The profiles, companies and photos in the demo are fictional and illustrative — the portraits are AI-generated.

4. Cookies and tracking

binder.red does not use cookies, local storage or similar technologies, and does not run analytics, advertising or social-media trackers. Fonts and icons are served from binder.red itself, so your browser does not contact third-party font or icon services. See the Cookie Policy.

  • Vercel Inc. (USA) — hosts the website and runs the contact form's server code; processes the technical data in section 2.
  • Resend (USA) — delivers contact-form submissions to our inbox by email.
  • [[EMAIL PROVIDER]] — hosts the inbox where your request is received and answered.

If we send you a link to book a call through a scheduling service, that service processes the booking under its own privacy policy, which it shows you before you book.

6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Vercel and Resend are based in the United States. Transfers to them rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards at the address in section 1.

7. How long we keep it

  • Contact requests and emails — for as long as needed to handle your request, then for up to 24 months after our last exchange so we have the context if you get back in touch. If you join the pilot or become a user, your data is then governed by the terms of that relationship.
  • Technical logs — kept by our hosting provider for a short period, as needed for security and operations.

8. Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR you can ask us at any time to:

  • confirm whether we hold data about you and give you a copy (access);
  • correct inaccurate data (rectification);
  • delete your data (erasure);
  • restrict how we use it;
  • give it to you in a portable format;
  • stop processing based on our legitimate interest (objection).

Write to [[PRIVACY EMAIL]]. We answer within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali — or with the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

9. Security

The website is served only over HTTPS. Contact-form data is sent from your browser to our server code and on to our inbox; it is not stored in a database on the website. Access to our inbox is limited to the Binder team.

10. Children

binder.red is meant for founders and investors and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data — for example if we add analytics — we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
